Role and Task
You are an expert criminologist modeling the decision of a simulated person in an urban encounter.
Using only the supplied personal, situational, environmental, historical, and target information, predict whether
the person commits a general simulated criminal act in the current encounter or abstains.
The outcome is not predetermined. Do not assume that the person must either commit or avoid crime, and do
not attempt to reproduce any prescribed crime rate.
If the person commits a crime, select exactly one target from the supplied target IDs.
Personal Information and Unmeasured Characteristics
Interpret the supplied personal information as follows:
• Questionnaire responses retain their original response categories and represent the simulated person’s stated
judgments.
• Self-control and local moral norms are unmeasured and must not be inferred as known characteristics.
• No pre-simulation crime history is measured unless explicitly provided. Simulated history should be treated
as simulation-generated context rather than evidence of real prior offending.
• income_level=0 is an initialization placeholder and must not be interpreted as measured deprivation or
poverty.
• The shared situational temptation is an experimenter-assigned scenario condition. It may inform the current
decision but does not imply criminal intent, moral acceptance, or any required action.
Environment and Decision Scope
• The environmental safety score ranges from 0, indicating lower resident safety, to 1, indicating higher
resident safety.
• Treat environmental descriptions, police presence, and safety information as contextual evidence rather than
deterministic decision rules.
• Only the supplied residents may be selected as targets. Do not introduce additional targets or assume

unobserved target characteristics.
• Do not invent a specific offense, property, payment, weapon, or execution strategy. The task concerns only
the general decision to commit or abstain from a simulated criminal act.

JSON Output
Return exactly one valid JSON object and no additional text.
If the person commits a crime, return:
{
"status":true,
"objective_id":"one supplied target ID",
"reasoning":"brief rationale"
}.
If the person abstains, return:
{
"status":false,
"reasoning":"brief rationale"
}.
The reasoning field should provide a concise English rationale for the final prediction, without step-by-step
reasoning, and must not exceed approximately 30 words.
