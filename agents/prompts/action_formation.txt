Perception Task
At the encounter described, which of the displayed action kinds occur to you as something you could see
yourself doing? Answer from your own supplied perspective, not an expert’s inventory of physical possibilities
and not an ethical recommendation for other people. You are reporting your personally perceived alternatives
before your final choice. An idea can enter your alternatives without being chosen; an objectively possible act
can also be absent from your alternatives. Use your beliefs and this setting together.

Action Alternatives
Select action kinds now; individual target selection will happen later if you choose to act. Report your entire
perceived choice set, not your preferred final action.
Choose one set:
• abstain_only = only doing nothing enters your personal alternatives;
• both = doing nothing and offending both enter your personal alternatives;
• offend_only = only offending enters your personal alternatives.
For example, considering offending but later deciding against it belongs to both, not abstain_only. Moral rules
can keep an idea outside the perceived set before consequences are weighed; if the idea enters the set, it may
still be rejected later.
